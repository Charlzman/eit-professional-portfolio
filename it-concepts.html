Presentation · Emerging technology

AI at Work

Created a presentation exploring how businesses can use artificial intelligence to support people rather than simply replace tasks. It explains common workplace uses of AI, opportunities for businesses, risks such as inaccurate outputs and bias, and skills workers may need as AI becomes more common.

The presentation finishes with a responsible AI playbook: start small, train people, keep human review, set clear rules and measure results. Its main message is that people should remain responsible for important decisions.
